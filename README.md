# 🚍 Employee Commute & Corporate Shuttle Optimization System

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end **Business Analysis project** focused on improving corporate employee transportation operations.

The project analyzes employee demand, shuttle routes, vehicle utilization, delays, cancellations, and occupancy to identify operational problems and provide data-driven recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Corporate transportation teams often face:

* Long employee waiting times
* Shuttle delays and cancellations
* Overcrowded or underutilized vehicles
* Inefficient routes
* Poor capacity planning
* Lack of centralized performance monitoring

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Identify high-demand pickup locations
* Improve vehicle and route utilization
* Reduce delays and waiting time
* Analyze cancellations and operational bottlenecks
* Improve capacity and route planning
* Support data-driven transportation decisions

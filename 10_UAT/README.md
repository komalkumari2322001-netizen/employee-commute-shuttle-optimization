# 🧪 User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

## 📌 Purpose

UAT validates whether the proposed transportation solution meets the defined business requirements and user expectations.

The testing covers demand analysis, vehicle utilization, waiting time, delays, cancellations, routes, costs, vendors and dashboard KPIs.

---

## 🎯 UAT Objectives

- Validate business requirements
- Verify expected functionality
- Check KPI calculations
- Validate dashboard information
- Identify defects
- Confirm business readiness

---

## 👥 UAT Participants

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Prepare test cases and coordinate UAT |
| Transport Manager | Validate transportation requirements |
| Management | Review KPIs and business outcomes |
| Finance | Validate cost-related information |

---

## 🔗 UAT Traceability

```text
Business Requirement
        ↓
Functional Requirement
        ↓
User Story
        ↓
Acceptance Criteria
        ↓
UAT Test Case
```

Example:

```text
BRQ-002
   ↓
FR-002
   ↓
US-002
   ↓
AC-002-01
   ↓
UAT-002
```

---

## 🧪 UAT Test Cases

The project contains 10 UAT test cases covering:

- Employee demand
- Vehicle utilization
- Capacity optimization
- Waiting time
- Delay and On-Time Performance
- Cancellation analysis
- Route and vehicle comparison
- Transportation cost
- Vendor performance
- Management dashboard

---

## ✅ Test Status

The following statuses are used:

- **Not Executed** – Test has not been performed
- **Pass** – Expected result is achieved
- **Fail** – Expected result is not achieved
- **Blocked** – Testing cannot continue due to another issue

---

## 🐞 Defect Management

Any issue identified during UAT is recorded in the Defect Log with:

- Defect ID
- Test Case ID
- Description
- Severity
- Status
- Resolution

---

## 📊 UAT Outcome

UAT will confirm whether the transportation solution meets the defined business requirements and is ready for business use.

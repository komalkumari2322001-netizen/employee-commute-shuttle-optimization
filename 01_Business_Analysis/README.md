# 🔍 Business Analysis

## 📌 Purpose

This section focuses on understanding the employee transportation business problem, identifying operational issues and defining areas for improvement.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The organization faces challenges such as:

- Long employee waiting times
- Shuttle delays and cancellations
- Vehicle overcrowding and underutilization
- Inefficient routes
- Poor capacity planning
- Limited KPI visibility
- Increasing transportation costs

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify high-demand pickup locations
- Improve vehicle utilization
- Optimize routes and vehicle capacity
- Reduce waiting time and delays
- Analyze cancellations and operational issues
- Improve transportation performance monitoring
- Support data-driven decision-making

## 👥 Stakeholder Analysis

Key stakeholders identified:

- Employees
- Transport Manager
- HR Team
- Admin / Facility Team
- Transport Vendors
- Finance Team
- Management

Stakeholders were analyzed based on their **interest, influence, needs, pain points and engagement strategy**.

## 🔎 Gap Analysis

The current **As-Is** state was compared with the desired **To-Be** state.

Key gaps identified:

- Lack of waiting-time monitoring
- Poor vehicle utilization visibility
- Limited demand-based route planning
- Difficulty tracking delays and cancellations
- Lack of centralized KPI reporting
- Limited cost and vendor performance visibility

## 🌳 Root Cause Analysis

Possible root causes identified include:

- Demand and schedule mismatch
- Capacity mismatch
- Inefficient routes
- Traffic-related delays
- Vehicle availability issues
- Limited performance monitoring
- Vendor performance issues

## 📋 Business Rules

Key business rules were defined for:

- Vehicle capacity
- Vehicle utilization
- Route planning
- Waiting time
- Delay monitoring
- Cancellation tracking
- KPI reporting
- Vendor performance

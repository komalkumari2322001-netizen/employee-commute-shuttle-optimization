# 🔍 Business Analysis

## 📌 Business Problem

Corporate transportation teams often face challenges in managing employee commute operations efficiently.

Common operational issues include:

- Long employee waiting times
- Shuttle delays and cancellations
- Overcrowded or underutilized vehicles
- Inefficient transportation routes
- Poor vehicle capacity planning
- Limited visibility into transportation performance
- Difficulty identifying high-demand pickup locations
- Lack of centralized KPI monitoring

These challenges can lead to increased transportation costs, inefficient vehicle utilization, employee dissatisfaction, and operational difficulties.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Identify high-demand pickup locations
- Analyze employee transportation demand
- Improve vehicle utilization
- Optimize shuttle routes and capacity
- Reduce employee waiting time
- Analyze delays and cancellations
- Identify operational bottlenecks
- Improve transportation performance monitoring
- Support data-driven transportation decisions

## 👥 Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role / Interest |
|---|---|
| Employees | Use transportation services and experience commute-related issues |
| Transport Manager | Manages routes, vehicles, schedules and transportation operations |
| HR Team | Monitors employee experience and transportation-related concerns |
| Admin / Facility Team | Coordinates transportation facilities and operations |
| Transport Vendors | Provide vehicles and transportation services |
| Finance Team | Monitors transportation costs and budgets |
| Management | Reviews performance and makes strategic decisions |

## 🔎 Key Business Questions

The analysis will answer questions such as:

1. Which pickup locations have the highest employee demand?
2. Which routes have low or high utilization?
3. Which vehicles are overcrowded or underutilized?
4. What is the average employee waiting time?
5. Which routes experience frequent delays?
6. What factors contribute to shuttle cancellations?
7. Where can routes or vehicle capacity be optimized?
8. How can transportation costs be reduced?
9. Which KPIs should management monitor regularly?

## 📊 Business Analysis Approach

The project will follow the following approach:

**Business Problem → Stakeholder Analysis → Requirement Gathering → Process Analysis → Data Analysis → Solution Recommendation**

## 💡 Expected Business Value

The proposed solution aims to help the organization:

- Improve employee commute experience
- Reduce waiting time and delays
- Improve vehicle utilization
- Optimize routes and capacity
- Reduce unnecessary transportation costs
- Improve operational visibility
- Support data-driven decision-making
